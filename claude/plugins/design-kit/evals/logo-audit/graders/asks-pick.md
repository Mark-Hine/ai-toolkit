PASS if the final message points the user to at least three logo options, labelled for example A, B and C, in files or on a board, and asks the user to choose, combine or reject them before the logo is replaced. The message may leave the options undescribed, because a blind board keeps them unexplained until the pick.
FAIL if the message reports that the logo was already replaced, offers fewer than three options, or does not ask the user to choose.
