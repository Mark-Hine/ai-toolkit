### D. Writing style (fixed in #18, #22)

- Em dashes 381 under `claude/` (pr-review packs 372, two iOS references, seven in `post_azdo.py` f-strings). En dashes 27, mostly numeric ranges. Semicolons throughout. "robust" once. Mirrors carry the same pack counts plus "honestly" five times.
- Hard wrapping is rare. Of 157 tracked markdown files, only four are wrapped near 120, and those are `writing-style.md` and the three `protocol.md` copies. The earlier claim that every rules file is wrapped was wrong.
- The rule file breaks its own rules (wrapped, parentheses, verbless openers) and contains two contradicting bold rules. The hook injects a one-line reminder, the full file loads once per session.
