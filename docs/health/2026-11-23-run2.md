## Top 3 findings

1. **Specialist agents are blocked by their own hook, and the main session then does the work.** Subagents took 23 of the 27 hook blocks. 2 judged sessions (`83d0d231`, `2c331f2e`) lost their device check this way. The hook rejected piped or chained commands, a path-qualified `adb` or `android`, and `adb install`. 19 of the 21 specialist blocks ran on 12 and 13 November. The guard has accepted a path-qualified CLI since 13 November, and the brief rule has stated the one-command limit since 2026-11-23, so both causes already have a fix that this window cannot measure.
2. **Closing replies are longer than the style rules allow.** The judges scored 11 of 12 sessions at 0.5 on verbosity for headings, tables, bold labels and recaps. In `e544e4e2` the person asked for shorter text. Dashes, semicolons and closing offers stay near 0, so the length rules are the ones not followed.
3. **Tests are good and test runs are still unfiltered.** The 4 sessions that wrote tests scored 0.75 or 1.0, with red-then-green tests that check behaviour. 37 of 66 Gradle test runs had no filter, and all 37 came from main sessions. 27 of the 37 ran on 12 and 13 November, before the filtered-run rule was added today.

## Proposed changes

None. Nothing here is applied.

The first version of this scorecard proposed a sentence for `shared/guidance/common.md` that stated the specialist limits. I withdrew it after rechecking it against the current `main`, for 3 reasons:

- `shared/guidance/common.md:32` already says that a researcher, reviewer or verifier specialist runs one plain command per call, with no chaining, redirection or substitution. That sentence landed on 2026-11-23.
- `shared/hooks/guard.py` has accepted an absolute or home path to `adb` and `android` since 13 November, and `shared/tests/test_guard.py:342` covers it. The path-qualified blocks in this window ran on 12 and 13 November.
- The verifier's install block already tells the caller what to do, and step 7 of the feature playbook says that the calling session builds, installs and launches the app.

I propose no diff for the style finding either. The style hook experiment is running, and a second change to the style rules would hide its result.
